What I offer

Bridal
Services

Bridal make up only — for the bride, her bridal party and the people closest to her. Every wedding is quoted individually, so nothing here is off-the-shelf.

The package

Your bridal
booking

My bridal package is designed to make your wedding morning feel relaxed, organised and completely stress-free. It includes your bridal trial, make up for the bride, and make up for additional members of your bridal party, with the option to add extra people if you need to.

Pricing is bespoke and depends on your party size and travel, so I'll send full package details and a quote when you enquire. Travel is included within ninety minutes of me; anything further afield may carry an overnight charge, which I'll always confirm up front.

How it works

From enquiry
to aisle

Enquire

Send your wedding date, getting-ready location and number in your bridal party through the enquiry form or by email.

Quote and secure the date

I'll check availability and send a bespoke quote with full package details. A small deposit secures your date, along with a booking contract.

Your bridal trial

Usually two to three months before the wedding. We'll talk through your ideas, get to know your skin and tweak until we land your look. A second small payment is due at the trial.

Your timeline

Once I know your party size and ceremony time, I'll build a morning timeline for you. The remaining balance is due forty-eight hours before.

The morning

I travel to you. Everyone is finished at least an hour before you leave, leaving time for touch-ups, photographs and getting into dresses.
