About
Helen

Eighteen years in the beauty industry, thirteen of them with MAC Cosmetics, and a Belfast diary built almost entirely on weddings.

Hi, I'm Helen. With eighteen years of experience in the beauty industry, including thirteen years with MAC Cosmetics, I've had the privilege of extensive makeup training and a wealth of experience within the bridal industry.

I pride myself on understanding each client's individual style and beauty preferences, working with you to create a bridal look that leaves you feeling like you — only elevated. Whether you're after soft and natural, glowing and elegant, or something a little more glamorous, we'll land on something that feels completely you.

My style is all about enhancing your natural beauty and creating a polished, timeless look that photographs flawlessly while lasting through all the dancing, happy tears and unforgettable moments of your day.

A huge part of being a makeup artist is adapting and evolving with changing trends, which is why I'm continually developing my skills and maintaining a luxury, professional kit that lets me create a wide range of looks across all skin types and tones.

My years of bridal experience have also taught me how important it is to stay calm, organised and adaptable, even when the pressure is on. I'll bring the calm — and the glam — to your wedding morning, keeping your timeline on track so you can relax, enjoy the moment and soak it all in.

It is an absolute privilege to play a small part in such an important day, and my priority is always that you feel one hundred per cent confident, beautiful and completely yourself.
